FAQ

What's the shortcut?
⌘⌥P opens the prompt picker from anywhere on your Mac.

Does it work in all apps?
Yes, any text field or editor—Safari, VS Code, Terminal, Messages.

How do I add prompts?
Copy text, hit ⌘N in Prompt Library, or drag from anywhere.

Is data stored securely?
Fully local. No cloud, no accounts. Your prompts stay on your Mac.
